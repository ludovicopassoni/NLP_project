EMNLP (Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing) is a 
premier conference covering empirical methods and applications 
in natural language processing. Relevant topics include 
computational social science and cultural analytics, dialogue 
and interactive systems, discourse and pragmatics, low-resource 
methods for NLP, ethics bias and fairness, text generation, 
information extraction, information retrieval and text mining, 
interpretability and analysis of models for NLP, linguistic 
theories and cognitive modeling, machine learning for NLP, 
machine translation, multilinguality and language diversity, 
multimodality and language grounding, phonology morphology and 
word segmentation, question answering, resources and evaluation, 
semantics and textual inference, sentiment analysis and argument 
mining, speech processing, summarization, syntax tagging and 
parsing, and NLP applications.
